# Economic unit cost source material

The official CY2022 CMS source files were recovered and checked on October 30, 2026. The source-document gap for the numerical inputs is now resolved. The implemented values remain $634.96 per composite CTA and $10.18 per quantitative D-dimer test. No economic analysis or result was rerun or changed.

Start with `CMS_CY2022/VERIFIED_COST_COMPONENTS.csv`. `CMS_CY2022/SOURCE_INDEX.csv` supplies official URLs, exact file members or page locators, and SHA-256 hashes. The original downloaded ZIP, PDF and HTML files are retained unchanged in that folder.

The composite calculation is $444.56 + $106.24 + 0.95 × $88.59 = $634.9605, rounded to $634.96. The facility component is APC 8006, CT and CTA with Contrast Composite, in the January 2022 OPPS correction Addendum A. Professional values are national unadjusted calculations for CPT 74174-26 and 71275-26 using the January 2022 RVU file and conversion factor, with geographic indices set to 1. They agree with the two older national-labelled professional-component extracts retained in this folder. The 2022 RVU rows carry multiple-procedure indicator 4. CMS CR9647 documents the 5% reduction for eligible additional professional components.

The composite payment and professional reduction conditions remain modeling assumptions, not confirmed claim-level eligibility for every encounter. In particular, same-session and applicable physician or group conditions must not be presented as established for the complete cohort. The existing aggregate timing/interpreter audit does not establish billing-group identity. The no-professional-reduction total is $639.39, retained in the existing analysis record as an alternative assumption, not substituted for the approved model.

The January 2022 CLFS row for CPT 85379 confirms $10.18. Its OPPS Addendum B status is Q4. CMS describes conditional packaging of Q4 laboratory services on qualifying outpatient claims. The CLFS value therefore serves as a standardized laboratory unit-cost proxy and does not establish separate incremental hospital outpatient reimbursement.
